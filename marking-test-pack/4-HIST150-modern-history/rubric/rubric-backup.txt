Title: Causes of the First World War
Question: Explain two causes of the First World War and assess which was more significant.

Criterion 1: First cause (2 marks)
0: Missing or incorrect.
1: Names a valid cause with little explanation.
2: Explains a valid cause (e.g. the alliance system, militarism, imperial rivalry, nationalism) and how it led to war.
Practice: How did the alliance system turn a local crisis into a European war?

Criterion 2: Second cause (2 marks)
0: Missing or incorrect.
1: Names a second valid cause with little explanation.
2: Explains a second, different cause and how it led to war.
Practice: Explain one way the naval race increased tension between Britain and Germany.

Criterion 3: Judgement (2 marks)
0: No judgement.
1: States which cause was more significant without support.
2: Makes a supported judgement that compares the two causes.
Practice: What evidence would show that one cause mattered more than another?
